About Discover Salone

We help visitors plan trips in Sierra Leone and help local guides and hotels reach them.

Why we built this

Information about Sierra Leone's destinations is spread across social media posts, group chats and word of mouth. Prices are unclear, listings go out of date and it is hard to know which guide to trust.

Discover Salone puts destinations, price estimates, directions and reviews in one place, with a direct way to contact local businesses.

117,000+

visitors came to Sierra Leone in 2024, with tourism supporting around 49,600 jobs. Source: World Bank blog, "Unlocking Sierra Leone's tourism potential".

How we verify listings

1. Check the business

We confirm the name, location and what the business offers.

2. Test the contact details

Phone numbers and emails must work before a listing goes live.

3. Review prices

Prices are checked again each season and show their last check date.
