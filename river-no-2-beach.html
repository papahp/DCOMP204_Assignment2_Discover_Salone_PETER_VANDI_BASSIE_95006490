River No. 2 Beach

A wide beach where a river meets the sea, known for fresh fish grilled on the sand.

Region
Western Area
Best for
Beach and seafood
Best season
November to April

How to get there

Map: Freetown to River No. 2 Beach
  1. Take a shared taxi from Freetown towards Lumley and the peninsula road.
  2. Ask the driver for the River No. 2 turn-off.
  3. Walk down to the beach and agree prices before ordering food.
