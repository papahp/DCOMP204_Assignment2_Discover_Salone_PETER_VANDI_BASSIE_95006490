Tokeh Beach

A long sandy beach on the Freetown Peninsula, about an hour and a half from the city.

Region
Western Area
Best for
Beach and relaxing
Best season
November to April

Price estimate

Last checked Sep 2026. Prices change by season and are sample figures.

How to get there

Map: Freetown to Tokeh Beach
  1. Take a shared taxi from Freetown to the peninsula road.
  2. Ask for Tokeh junction and continue to the beach.
  3. Arrange a return ride before the afternoon.
