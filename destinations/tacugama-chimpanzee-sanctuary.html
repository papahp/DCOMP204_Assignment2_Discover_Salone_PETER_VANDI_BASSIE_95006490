Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary

A sanctuary in the hills above Freetown that cares for rescued chimpanzees and runs guided visits.

How to get there

Map: Freetown to Tacugama
  1. Take a taxi from central Freetown towards Regent.
  2. Follow the signs for the sanctuary road.
  3. Book the visit in advance and arrive at the start time.
