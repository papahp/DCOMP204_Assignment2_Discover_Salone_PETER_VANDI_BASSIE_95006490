Tiwai Island

A river island wildlife sanctuary in the south where visitors can walk forest trails with a local guide.

How to get there

Map: Freetown to Tiwai Island
  1. Travel by road to the Southern Province and the river landing.
  2. Cross the river by canoe with your guide.
  3. Plan an overnight stay so you can walk at dawn.
